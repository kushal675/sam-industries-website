Inspection & Testing

Advanced Metrology Lab.

Our in-house metrology lab is equipped with coordinate measuring machines (CMM), hardness testers, surface roughness testers and other precision instruments — ensuring every dimension, tolerance and finish meets specification.

Trained quality engineers audit every batch, and calibration schedules keep our instruments accurate at all times.
