Company Overview

About SAM Industries

Established in 1997, SAM Industries was founded with a clear vision to manufacture precision-engineered railway components that deliver the highest standards of safety, quality, and reliability.

Today, we are a trusted manufacturing partner to Indian Railways, rolling stock manufacturers, and customers across international markets. Our products support railway operations worldwide, reflecting nearly three decades of engineering expertise, manufacturing excellence, and long-standing customer confidence.

From a small engineering enterprise, SAM Industries has evolved into one of Eastern India's leading manufacturers of critical railway brake system components. Our growth has been driven by continuous investment in advanced manufacturing technologies, skilled people, and robust quality systems, enabling us to meet the evolving requirements of modern railway networks.

Our integrated manufacturing facility brings together CNC & VMC machining, fabrication, assembly, testing and comprehensive quality assurance under one roof. Every component is manufactured with precision, inspected rigorously, and validated to meet demanding railway specifications before it reaches our customers.

At SAM Industries, we believe that lasting partnerships are built on consistent quality, dependable delivery, and continuous innovation. These principles continue to guide our commitment to supporting safer, more efficient, and more reliable railway transportation across India and around the world.

Our Achievements Over the Years

• Developed and manufactured RDSO approved Components.

• We executed a prestigious export order for the supply of 1,300 Vacuum Brake Cylinders to Myanmar Railways, reinforcing our expertise in delivering mission-critical railway components to international markets.

• Achieved an estimated 95% market share in DOM Cylinders for BOBRN wagons supplied to Indian Railways.

• Among the first manufacturers in India to automate the CNC machining of critical air brake valve viz angle cock, dirt collector etc. improving precision and consistency.

• Our in house ERP system reflects our commitment to technology-driven efficiency, control, and continuous improvement.

• Built a trusted global presence by consistently supplying quality railway products across Asia, Europe, Africa, and Latin America.

• Established best-in-class testing facilities for critical railway components, including an automated testing system developed in-house to deliver greater accuracy, consistency, and reliability.
